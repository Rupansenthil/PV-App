Assumptions used
  • Regional yield figures approximate typical PVWatts-style output (kWh/kWp/year) — real output varies with shading, panel quality, and inverter losses.
  • Monthly split uses a fixed seasonal curve (summer-peaked) applied to the annual total — not location-specific month-by-month data.
  • Grid emission factors are approximate national averages by state and change over time as the grid decarbonises.
  • Savings assume no battery storage; exported energy is paid at the feed-in tariff, self-used energy offsets the full retail rate.
  • Excludes maintenance, inverter replacement, and financing costs.
